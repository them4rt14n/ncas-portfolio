Executive Summary

I'm Marvin Segismundo, a first-generation college student studying engineering at City College of San Francisco, or CCSF. My career goal is aerospace design engineering for Advanced Air Mobility, the new kind of air travel built around electric vertical takeoff and landing aircraft, or eVTOL. I want to design and test the structures and parts that make these aircraft light, safe, and buildable.

I picked this path because it matches what I already do. I model parts in SOLIDWORKS for Lipad, a small eVTOL quadplane I'm designing on my own. I helped build the solar panel frame for my Engineering Club's boat, which took 2nd place among colleges at the California Solar Regatta. Right now my team is building a robot that launches tennis balls, and we use MATLAB to work out each launch. Lipad eVTOL Quadplane

Personal design project · SOLIDWORKS · 2026 to now

DESIGN

Skills I used

Lipad means "fly" in Tagalog, and it's a lift-plus-cruise quadplane with four fixed lift motors and a rear pusher propeller. I'm building it part by part in SOLIDWORKS. So far I've modeled seven parts, including all four motor pylons, the nose and main landing gear, and an equipment tray. Each pylon has a four-bolt pattern for its motor, and I set a weight budget so every part has a target to stay under.

How it fits NASA

NASA's Revolutionary Vertical Lift Technology project studies a Lift+Cruise concept aircraft built on the same idea, with separate lift rotors for hover and a pusher propeller for cruise.4 The NASA concept carries six passengers and has eight lift rotors, so Lipad is a much smaller cousin of it. Working through motor mounts, landing gear, and weight on my own design is practice for the vehicle design work that supports Advanced Air Mobility.5

Tennis Roboreturn

Team project · CCSF course ENGN 10B · Fall 2026

TEST

Skills I used

Tennis Roboreturn is my team's final project in ENGN 10B, which is CCSF's Introduction to Engineering: Software Tools and Design course. We're building an autonomous robot that launches tennis balls at targets. I'm helping design the launcher, and we use MATLAB to calculate the launch angle and speed before we test. The full robot is also modeled as a CAD assembly, so we can check how the parts fit before we build.

How it fits NASA

O*NET says aerospace engineers plan and run tests on models and prototypes, and this project is that on a small scale.2 We predict where the ball should land, launch it, measure the miss, and adjust. MATLAB is also one of the software tools O*NET lists for this career, so I'm learning it on a real problem.2

Solar Regatta Boat Frame

CCSF Engineering Club · SMUD California Solar Regatta · Spring 2026

BUILD

Skills I used

In spring 2026 I joined the CCSF Engineering Club team that built a solar-powered boat for the SMUD California Solar Regatta. My part was helping build the frame that holds the solar panel on the boat. The frame had to keep the panel steady on the water without adding much weight. We took 2nd place in the college division on May 2, 2026.

How it fits NASA

A frame that carries a load without extra weight is the same problem aircraft structures have to solve. The boat also ran on electric power, and NASA's vertical lift research is focused on electric propulsion for the next generation of aircraft.5 Racing against other colleges taught me to build with a team on a deadline, which is how engineering projects at NASA run too.
